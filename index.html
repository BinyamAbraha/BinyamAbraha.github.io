About Me

Builder. Thinker.
Lifelong Learner.

I'm Binyam Abraha — a senior CS student at Cal State East Bay, future AI & Machine Learning grad student, and someone who genuinely can't stop building things. My curiosity runs deep: I want to understand how things work at the base level, which is what pulled me into tech and keeps me sharp across software engineering, data, robotics, and AI.

Outside of code, you'll find me training Muay Thai, hiking, cooking, or tending to my garden — hobbies that all share the same theme: discipline, patience, and showing up consistently. That same mindset drives my technical work.

Long term, I want to push my potential as far as it goes in data and robotics, and eventually open my own restaurant — because my love for feeding people is just as real as my love for engineering. I also hope to open a school, library, and gym back home in Tigray, Ethiopia. Whatever I build, I want it to matter.
